## Productivity dashboard

I was tired of heavy, slow productivity apps that take forever to load just to write down a quick task. Most of them ask for logins or try to charge monthly fees.

I wanted something fast and simple tailored specifically for college life: tracking assignments, scheduling classes, and timing study sessions. Using AI as a coding assistant, I built this dashboard into a single file that opens instantly.

## Features

- Assignment Tracker: Keep track of your homework by subject. Color tags show you what is due soon.
- Focus Timer: A built-in study timer with daily stats to keep track of your study streak.
- Weekly Schedule: A class timetable that shows your next upcoming class with a countdown timer.
- Quick Scratchpad: Simple sticky notes to write down lecture notes, code snippets, or quick ideas.
- Habits and Goals: Track daily routines with an automatic midnight reset so you start fresh the next day.
- 100% Private: Everything stays on your computer inside your browser storage. No personal information is sent anywhere. You can download a backup copy of your data anytime or clear it with one click.
